Pause before you react

STEP 2

Context notes

Context adds information. It does not tell participants what to believe.

SHARED CONTEXT

Different lived stakes can be true at the same time

A temporary operational measure may feel like a safety response to one audience and an immediate loss of mobility to another. The experience depends on who travels, who waits, and what alternatives are available.

What to ask
Who is affected, for how long, and what information would reduce uncertainty?
What to avoid
Turning one headline into a claim about an entire community’s motives.

Pod guidelines

  1. Ask about the event, not about a group.
  2. Say what you don’t know.
  3. Cite the source record when you make a claim.
  4. Report anything that targets a person or a people.
