Every mark here is one inline SVG, so it scales to any size and needs no asset files. Set B builds on the bilingual wordmark you already have; Set A predates it. Tell me which one you want and I will wire it into index.html and export a standalone SVG plus a favicon.

Set B — built on your bilingual mark

Your existing logo is Arabic جسر and Hebrew גשר — both bridge — superimposed so they share their three-pronged middle letter (س / ש). The idea is strong and I have not touched it. What it lacks is a small size: at 28px in the sidebar, or at 16px in a browser tab, two overlapping words in two scripts turn to mush. So these are icons that carry the same idea, meant to sit alongside the wordmark rather than replace it.

The three prongs both scripts have in common, drawn twice in your two colours and offset — the same superimposition as the original, reduced to the one shape that survives at icon size.
